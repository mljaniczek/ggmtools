# TCGA breast cancer PI3K pathway expression, Normal vs Tumor

RNA-seq expression of the 28 PI3K-pathway genes (Sanchez-Vega et al.
2018) in 100 normal breast tissue samples and 100 primary breast tumours
from The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) BRCA cohort. This is the dataset of
the TCGA example in the DiNA simulation paper. It is shipped here so
that spikeyglass, multiGGM and other packages use one identical object.

## Usage

``` r
brca_pi3k
```

## Format

A named list of two numeric matrices, `Normal` and `Tumor`, each 100
samples x 28 genes, with HGNC gene symbols as column names (in the same
order in both). The attribute `"genes"` is a data frame with the columns
`Gene`, `og_tsg` (oncogene / tumour suppressor / other, from
Sanchez-Vega et al.) and `pathway`.

## Source

The Cancer Genome Atlas, via the Bioconductor package curatedTCGAData
(Ramos et al. 2020). The results here are in whole or in part based upon
data generated by the TCGA Research Network:
<https://www.cancer.gov/tcga>.

## Details

Built by `data-raw/brca_pi3k.R` in the package sources:

1.  curatedTCGAData 2.1.1, BRCA RNASeqGene assay
    (`BRCA_RNASeqGene-20160128`, raw counts). Tumour = TCGA sample type
    01, Normal = 11; the 3 metastatic samples are dropped.

2.  Genes with a count of at least 10 in fewer than 20\\ dropped.

3.  DESeq2 variance-stabilising transformation (`vst(blind = FALSE)`,
    design `~ sample_type`).

4.  Each gene is z-scored across *all* Normal and Tumour samples
    together. The groups are therefore **not** standardised separately:
    within group, the Normal genes have standard deviations of about 0.7
    and the Tumour genes about 1. Standardise within group (e.g.
    `spikeyglass::ssjgl(standardize = TRUE)`) if your method needs it.

5.  Normal samples, and tumours of patients without a paired normal
    sample, are kept, so the two groups come from different patients.

6.  The PI3K-pathway genes of Sanchez-Vega et al. (2018) are kept (28 of
    29 pass the filters).

7.  The first 100 tumours (in the order produced by the pipeline, not a
    random sample) are kept to balance the 100 normals.

Sample identifiers are not included.
